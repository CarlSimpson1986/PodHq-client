My Fit Pod is the companion app for your gym -- book a session, unlock the door with your phone, and train with a personalised coach that adapts to you.

BOOK & ACCESS
See real-time availability at your home gym or any gym in the network, book in seconds, and unlock the door with GPS-verified access right from your phone -- no key fob needed.

TRAINING THAT ADAPTS
Get a structured strength programme with rest-timer guidance, session tracking, and a coach that notices when a lift is declining and flags it before it becomes a problem.

NUTRITION & HEALTH
Log meals by search or barcode scan, track your daily habits, and sync steps, sleep, and heart-rate data from Health Connect to see how recovery affects your training.

MEMBERSHIPS & CREDITS
Manage your membership, top up credit packs, and buy or gift sessions, all from within the app.

My Fit Pod is built for members of participating gyms -- you will need an active membership or credits at a partner gym to book sessions.
